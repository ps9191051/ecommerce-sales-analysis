# E-Commerce Sales Analysis

An end-to-end Data Analyst project built using **MySQL** and **Power BI** to analyze 20,000+ e-commerce orders and uncover customer, product, and regional sales insights.

## Project Overview

This project transforms raw transactional data into interactive business dashboards. The analysis focuses on revenue trends, customer behavior, product performance, discounts, and regional sales.

## Tech Stack

* **SQL (MySQL)** – Data extraction & analysis
* **Power BI** – Dashboard & KPI visualization
* **Excel** – Data cleaning & validation

## Business Questions Solved

* What is the total revenue, orders, customers, and units sold?
* Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
* Which regions perform best?
* Who are the top customers by revenue?
* Which products sell the most units?
* How do discounts impact revenue?

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Overview

* KPI Cards
* Monthly Revenue Trend
* Revenue by Category
* Revenue by Region
* Orders by Category

### 2. Customer Analytics

* Top Customers by Revenue
* Average Revenue per Customer
* Average Orders per Customer
* Discount vs Revenue
* Interactive Region & Category Filters

### 3. Product & Performance Analytics

* Revenue by Subcategory
* Quantity by Category
* Top Products by Quantity
* Average Order Value by Discount

## Key Insights

* Revenue exceeded **₹2.9 Billion** across the analyzed dataset.
* Electronics contributed the highest category revenue.
* West region generated the strongest regional sales.
* Revenue declined consistently at higher discount levels.
